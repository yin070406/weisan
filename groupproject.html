    SDT Logic & Ethical Notes

    Self-Determination Theory (SDT): Missions 1–2 unlock the Competence Key, Mission 3 unlocks the Relatedness Key, Mission 4 unlocks the Autonomy Key, and Mission 5 integrates all three needs in Reflection Hall.

    Important Scope & Care Notice: This single-player educational game explores first-semester academic stress through fictional scenarios. Completing the game represents expected learning awareness only and cannot replace professional counselling, academic advising, or real-life peer support. If you experience persistent distress, please contact your university's Student Counselling Service.
